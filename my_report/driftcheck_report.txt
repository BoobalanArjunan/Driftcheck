How to read this: 'offset' is the number of seconds to ADD to the label times to match the audio. It is found by lining up where the labels say there is speech with where the audio has sound, using a simple energy detector (no speech model). It works when speech stands out from the background and needs a minute or more of audio to be certain; on short clips it says 'could not be measured' instead of guessing. A PASS means none of these checks found a problem, not that every label is right.
